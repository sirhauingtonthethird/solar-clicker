01 / The project

A small game with a very large event.

Orbit Clicker is a compact idle game with the pacing of a bigger one. It pairs simple, immediate feedback with long-term progression and two rare moments designed to be remembered.

The loop begins with a single orb. Every tap gathers stardust, charges the singularity meter, and feeds a growing chain multiplier. Spend what you earn on click power, orbital drones, critical hits, and the odds of a solar surge.

Built as a student project, it is also a study in game feel: how a ripple, a rising counter, a glitch, and a thirty-second change of rules can make a browser tab feel like a universe.

“The best moment in a clicker is when the rules suddenly change.” — Hayson, creator

03 / The signature event

500 clicks

The screen changes before the rules do

Thirty seconds that feel like another game.

At 500 charge, the page fades to black, a white flash breaks the frame, and the singularity opens. For thirty seconds, the whole interface glitches around a black-hole core while every click lands at ten times its final value. Then the flash returns, the meter resets, and the universe is yours again.

04 / How to play

Four moves. One growing universe.

No tutorial required. The loop reveals itself in seconds—then keeps deepening.

01

Tap the core

Click or tap the orb, or press Space. Faster clicks build a stronger chain multiplier.

02

Buy your edge

Spend stardust on five upgrade modules. Choose burst, automation, criticals, or rare-event luck.

03

Chase the events

Solar Surges reward patience. The Singularity rewards persistence. Both rewrite the math.

04

Leave the drones on

Close the tab. Your orbital drones keep working for up to four hours, and your progress autosaves.

First move

Your first Click Capacitor costs 20 stardust—about twenty taps. After that, the engine starts to compound.

06 / Good to know

The short answers.

Everything you need before your first click—saving, devices, silent play, and what waits at 500.

Play the game
Is Orbit Clicker free?
Yes—completely free, with no account or download. Open the game and tap.
Does my progress save?
Automatically, in your browser's local storage. In private browsing or when storage is blocked, progress may only last the session.
Can I play on my phone?
Yes. The layout, core, shop, and language controls are responsive. Use tap instead of Space.
What happens at 500 clicks?
The singularity opens: the screen fades, flashes, and runs thirty seconds of purple chaos with tenfold click power. The meter then resets.
Does the game have sound?
No. It is intentionally silent—no music, no effects, no voiceover.
